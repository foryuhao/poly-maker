# V4: paired maker liquidity provision

Registered after V2/V3 training selections were null; neither prior holdout is used as validation for this new hypothesis. New independently discovered universe is liquidity_universe_20261031_1900 (top1000 volume markets, current spread>=2ticks, max2 contracts per parent event). No live orders.

## Economic hypothesis

A maker entry followed by a mandatory taker exit mechanically pays an exit spread when prices stay unchanged. Test providing liquidity on BOTH economic legs, with a hard finite hedge deadline to retain adverse inventory outcomes. This is a different hypothesis, not a retrofit of V3.

Policies: neutral_inside and pressure_filtered (near-depth imbalance>=.5 and no primary bid decline in previous3s). Primary first outcome per condition. Five-share BUY at bid+tick strictly inside spread>=2ticks; price.02..98. At most one owner per market/policy/horizon/delay, new entry decisions at most every15s. Entry live lease60s; manual cancel latency modeled. Post/cancel latency scenarios .5,1,2s. Quotes must still strictly improve bid and not cross ask at simulated venue arrival; no imagined queue priority. Timing uncertainty, late source frames, tick changes, resolution and stream-break exposures use conservative V3-style loss-only bounds.

After a positively observed full five-share entry, quote BUY of the complementary token at its bid+tick, strictly inside its current spread. Pairing fixes total payout1 per pair without relying on delivery of newly purchased tokens for a SELL. This models the capital-funded hedge actually available before chain confirmation. Initial collateral reserve16USDC per owner covers entry, partial complementary fills and a minimum-size fallback order; do not recycle capital until both legs' simulated release buffer5s has passed.

Place at most one complementary maker order at a time, fixed price, initial five shares. If no maker hedge has been posted because spread/priority guards fail, retry on book updates no more frequently than every5s. Once admitted the hedge quote stays at its original price until all five shares fill or the hedge deadline. Do NOT cancel it on a partial hedge fill; partial executions remain exposed. No repricing after adverse moves. Hedge deadline30s or120s from first entry fill; cancel outstanding hedge and drain through modeled cancel arrival+2.25s. Then buy the remaining complement aggressively at full-depth ask VWAP. Minimum fallback order size5 shares: if a nonzero remainder is below5, pay for5, count excess complementary tokens at zero value in the lower-bound PnL. Entry partials below5 also remain in PnL; if minimum hedge execution cannot be supported, mark at zero recovery, never discard them.

Strict maker hedge fills require complementary SELL or primary BUY trade-through, canonicalized/deduplicated to prevent mirror volume multiplication. Price credited is our quote, never the better reported price. Source/receipt guards and same-connection requirements apply. Ambiguous shadow crossings on the hedge are alternative possible executions, NOT favorable credits: use a conservative minimum-PnL bound over feasible fill quantities when liquidating at the deadline. A full five-share strict execution through one original fixed-price hedge quote establishes the five-share paired cost even if some earlier ambiguous fill would have replaced part of it. Late post-only admission uncertainty cannot create a positive return.

Reserve0.004USDC/share per completed position (includes execution/rounding and0.01USDC fixed merge/redemption reserve for5 shares); report double-reserve sensitivity. Capital hurdle8% annual applied to actual holding/release period. Unknown costs ineligible. Public counterfactual fills are not actual exchange fills; separate fill-assumption audit required.

Implementation clarification before any V4 return inspection: buying a minimum5 complementary shares to cover a tiny remainder can cost more than the entire unhedged remainder. This is strictly dominated by accepting the remainder's maximum possible loss. At the hedge deadline choose the larger GUARANTEED net value of (a) minimum-size forced hedge and (b) merge/retain known pairs and write off the remaining primary shares at zero. This choice uses current executable prices and private filled quantity, not future outcomes. For public ambiguous fills, take the minimum across feasible filled quantities of this policy's guaranteed value, including its interior kink, not merely endpoint quantities. A possible partial entry below minimum can be stranded, so ambiguous entry bounds include full entry-cost loss even if a hypothetical full entry would pair profitably. Reserve16USDC per position; test a100USDC initial per-market cash buffer against prefix drawdown plus active reserve rather than assume unlimited reinvestment. Known paired collateral release gets a5s settlement buffer. These are causal solvency/risk-dominance corrections, not selected on performance.

Causal fallback execution clarification before return evaluation: the risk-dominance choice is implemented as a FOK BUY whose per-share limit is derived from the remaining guaranteed payoff and effective fees at decision time. Credit fallback only if the entire five-share book quantity is available at or below that cap on arrival; average VWAP alone cannot authorize consuming a higher-priced final level. Use actual depth VWAP for paid cost after admission. An unfillable capped FOK writes off residual exposure rather than buying at an unknown future price. For ambiguous hedge quantities, evaluate endpoints and the cap-admission kink (including the adjacent minimum share increment) to bound its possibly discontinuous return function.

Before-return accounting details: impose15s cool-down after each closed/released position. Test100USDC initial cash against16USDC maximum active reserve plus prior realized lower-bound losses; reject candidates needing more cash instead of dropping their losing positions. Report allocated100USDC annual8% opportunity cost as an additional conservative capital sensitivity, even though per-position holding costs are already charged. Final evidence audit must verify settlement status of the real public transactions underlying credited strict fills (when transaction hashes exist), then separately discuss why a counterfactual quote would have intercepted them. Public trade messages alone do not prove successful chain settlement or actual research-account fills.

Buy-fee delivery correction before any V4 return evaluation: official maker-rebate/fee documentation states that taker BUY fees, although valued in USD, are collected in outcome shares. A gross buy of5 need not deliver5 hedge tokens. For a supported rate r<.2 and exponent>=1, the share-fee fraction is bounded above by r. Fallback buys gross ceil_0.01(5/(1-r)) shares (5.38 at r=.07), guaranteeing at least5 net shares at all eligible prices. Walk depth for that larger gross quantity, fund its gross cash spend, and do not deduct the same fee again in USD. Mark excess net tokens at zero. The causal FOK cap uses this gross quantity's worst consumed price; unsupported fee exponents disallow fallback. This conservative policy can miss profitable smaller hedges but cannot invent delivered tokens. Source: https://help.polymarket.com/en/articles/13364471-maker-rebates-program .

Fee-rounding robustness refinement before return inspection (supersedes the previous5.38 example): per-maker-fill fee rounding may be unknown from L2. Rounding a positive nominal fee to nearest0.00001, with sub-half-unit values zero, can at most double the nominal fee. For supported r<.2,e>=1 use gross ceil_0.01(5/(1-2r)) (5.82 at r=.07), guaranteeing5 net shares even under the conservative per-fill rounding envelope. Actual fees may be much lower and extra tokens have value, but neither is credited in this lower-bound screen. Zero-rate markets remain exactly5 shares. Do not call the envelope the actual fee rate; confirm credited public fills' effective fees/onchain settlement if a candidate survives.
